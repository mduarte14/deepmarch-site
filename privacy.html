Privacy Policy

Endless Dungeon, by the Endless Dungeon team. Last updated: 26 October 2026.

Short version

  • The game is fully playable offline. Your save and settings stay on your device.
  • The game only sends data if you choose Join on the global leaderboards. That is optional, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • If you do join, we store your display name, class and run results under a random player ID. We do not ask for or collect your email address, real name, contacts, location or advertising ID.
  • The game shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. We never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete everything we store about you, at any time, from inside the game.

1. Data stored on your device

The game keeps your save file (characters, items, progress, achievements), settings, a local leaderboard and log files on your device. Nothing of this leaves the device unless you send it yourself, for example by using Copy bug report info or Open logs folder in Settings and sharing it with us.

2. Global leaderboards (optional)

When you choose Join (Leaderboard screen or Settings → Account → Global leaderboard), the game creates an anonymous player identity on our leaderboard server and, from then on, sends your runs when you return to town and when you die.

What is sent and stored:

  • a random player ID (an anonymous account created by the game; shown in game as a short tag like #a1b2);
  • the display name you chose in the game (shown publicly on the leaderboards; please don't use your real name if you don't want it to be public);
  • run results: class, distance reached, whether a waygate was used, level, kills, elite kills, bosses killed, chests opened, run time, a random run ID and the time the run ended;
  • technical details needed to keep the boards fair: game version, whether it is a debug build, and your platform (Windows or Android).

What is not sent: email, real name, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, your save file or your items.

Why: to show global rankings, your own rank, and to detect impossible or cheated runs (runs that fail the automatic plausibility checks are stored as rejected or flagged so the rules can be tuned, and rejected runs are deleted after 30 days).

Legal basis (for players in the EU/EEA/UK): your consent, given by choosing Join. You can withdraw it at any time by turning the global leaderboard off.

3. Where the data is stored

The leaderboard runs on Supabase, which acts as our data processor and hosts the data in the United States. All traffic between the game and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Supabase's own infrastructure may briefly log technical request data such as IP addresses for security and operations; we do not store IP addresses in our leaderboard tables. See Supabase's privacy policy.

4. How long we keep it

Your player record and accepted runs are kept while you take part in the leaderboards, until you delete them. Rejected runs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop sending data: Settings → Account → turn off Global leaderboard.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Account → Delete my online data immediately erases your player record and all your runs from the server. Reset online identity does the same and starts a fresh, unlinked ID.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your local save and the key that links you to your online record; online records you didn't delete then stay anonymous. You can still ask us to delete them (see Contact), ideally with your player tag and display name.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may also have the right to access, correct or port your data, to object, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Your display name, class and run results are shown publicly on the leaderboards; that is the purpose of the feature.

7. Links

The game links to our community Discord for feedback. Discord is a separate service with its own privacy policy; nothing is sent to it unless you open the link.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The leaderboards are optional and anonymous. If you believe a child has posted personal information as a display name, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, ask again in the game before sending any data.
